# Singles

Cover some of the squares with a {{pair:0}} square, in such a way that:

- no number appears twice in any row or column among the squares left
  clear
- no two {{pair:0}} squares are adjacent
- the clear squares form a single connected group (connections
  along diagonals do not count).

## Controls

{{controls}}

Either button, or either key, clears a square that is already covered
or circled.

A covered square hides its number. Clicking outside the grid shows
the numbers on the {{pair:0}} squares, and clicking there again hides
them; it is the same setting as the **Show numbers on {{pair:0}}
squares** preference.

## Hints

**Hint** explains the next step rather than simply making it. It reasons
only from the numbers and your own {{pair:0}} squares and circles, so it
carries on from wherever you are, as long as none of those is wrong; if
one is, it asks you to fix the highlighted mistakes first.

In its words, a *{{pair:0}}* square is one you have covered, and a
*clear* square is one that stays uncovered. When a step says a square
must be clear, record that with a circle (right-click); when it must be
{{pair:0}}, click it.

{{hint-marks}}

A few patterns are worth learning by name:

* **A sandwich.** Two equal numbers with one square between them: one of
  the two must be {{pair:0}}, so the square between them must be clear.
* **A touching pair.** Two equal numbers side by side: one of them stays
  clear, so every other copy of that number in their row or column must
  be {{pair:0}}.
* **Pairs in neighboring lines.** A pair of equal numbers in one row and
  another pair in the next, lined up so that covering either of two
  squares would force two {{pair:0}} squares side by side. Both must be
  clear.
* **Corners.** Where matching numbers crowd a corner of the grid, some
  ways of covering them would leave the corner square with no clear
  neighbor, so those are ruled out.
* **Keeping the clear squares joined.** A square that would cut the
  clear squares in two if {{pair:0}}, or seal a clear square off, must
  be clear.
