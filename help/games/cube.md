# Cube

Roll the cube around the grid, picking up the {{pair:0}} squares on its
faces. Try to get all the {{pair:0}} squares on to the object at the same
time, in as few moves as possible.

## Controls

Use the arrow keys to roll the cube, or 8, 2, 4 and 6 on the numeric
keypad, or click the mouse where you want it to roll towards. After every roll, the grid square and cube
face that you brought into contact swap their colors, so that a
plain cube face can pick up a {{pair:0}} square, but a {{pair:0}} face rolled
on to a plain square puts it down again.

On the triangular grids a triangle has one flat side, above or below it, and two sloping ones. Up or Down, whichever points at the flat side, rolls the solid across it, and Left and Right roll it across the sloping sides. The diagonal keys of the numeric keypad (7, 9, 1 and 3) make the same three rolls: the two that lean toward the flat side both cross it, and each of the other two crosses the sloping side it leans toward.

When you have mastered the cube, use the Type menu to select other
regular solids!
