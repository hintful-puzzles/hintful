# Guess

Try to guess the hidden combination of colors. You will be given
limited information about each guess you make, enabling you to
refine the next guess.

Drag from the colors on the left into the topmost unfilled row to
make a guess; then click on the small circles to submit that guess.
The small circles give you your feedback: black pegs indicate how
many of the colors you guessed were the right color in the right
place, and white pegs indicate how many of the rest were the right
colors but in the wrong place.

Or press one of the color buttons below the grid, which is the same
thing without the drag — each button is painted in the color it
places, and the digit on it is the key that does the same from the
keyboard. Tap a peg in the row you are filling to choose where the
next color goes, and the Clear button empties the peg you chose.

Right-click a peg in that row — or hold a finger on it — to *hold*
it, marking that color to be carried into your next guess. Press
**L** to number the colors, which can help when two of them are hard
to tell apart.

The keyboard can also be used. Move the cursor with the arrow keys:
left and right choose a peg, up and down choose a color. Press a
color's digit to place it, or Enter to place the color the cursor is
on. Backspace empties a peg and Space holds one. Once the row is
full the cursor can move one step past its last peg, where Enter
submits the guess. Pressing **H** fills the row with a combination
that fits every piece of feedback you have been given so far.
