# Skeleton

You have a grid of squares, some of which hold a number. Shade squares so that every number counts the shaded squares sharing a side with it. A numbered square is never shaded.

Every puzzle has exactly one solution, and none needs a guess.

## Controls

{{controls}}

You need not cross anything out to finish: the puzzle is solved as soon as every number counts the shaded squares beside it. A number turns red once the squares beside it can no longer meet it.

## Hints

**Hint** explains the next step rather than simply making it. Each step decides one or more squares from the numbers and from the squares you have already marked, so it carries on from wherever you are.

{{hint-marks}}

A step reads one number at a time where that is enough: a number that already has its shaded squares leaves the rest beside it clear, and one that needs every open square beside it has them all shaded. On Normal boards a step sometimes reads two numbers together, where every open square beside one of them is also beside the other.

If a square you marked is wrong, the hint asks you to fix the highlighted mistakes first.
