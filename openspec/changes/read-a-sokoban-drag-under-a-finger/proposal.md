# read-a-sokoban-drag-under-a-finger

**Status: scaffolded, not started (2026-10-04). Nothing here is owed: it is
the owner's to pick up or drop.** A follow-up from `walk-by-tap-push-by-drag`.

## Why

A push in Sokoban is a drag, and on a phone the finger covers the barrel and
the square it will stop on. The ghost barrel on the stopping square was added
for that. Owner, 2026-10-03, after it: *"definitely helps, but I'm not sure
it's enough. Let's leave it be for now, and I might come back to it later."*
The drag was then played on a real device and accepted as it stands
(2026-10-04), so this is an improvement to weigh, not a defect.

## What Changes

Nothing is decided. The ideas recorded so far:

- **An on-screen D-pad** in the app's keypad panel, sending the arrow keys the
  game already answers (a step at a time, pushing). Declined as a replacement
  for the drag, since it cannot push a barrel to a far square, which the owner
  likes; open as an addition beside it. Costs: screen space, and the panel's
  place on a phone.
- **An offset aim**: the barrel's stop trailing the finger, so it is never
  under it. Not explored.
- **A larger ghost** that shows around a fingertip. Not explored.

## Acceptance

How a drag feels under a finger, so the owner's, on a real device.
