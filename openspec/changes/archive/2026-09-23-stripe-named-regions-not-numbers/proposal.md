# stripe-named-regions-not-numbers

## Why

Owner, 2026-09-23, accepting `hatch-the-line-a-hint-counts`: *"I'd actually
advise to go ahead with both of these followups. I don't see any benefit in
reaching to rows and columns with numbers that are not present on the board
itself, and would prefer the stripes throughout."*

The two follow-ups were: a sentence naming a **region** ("this cage", "this
block", "the outlined region of 5") still outlined it, so the outline meant a
region in some steps and particular cells in others; and some sentences name a
line by a **number the board does not draw** ("Row 3 still needs…", "take it
to column 4"), which sends the player counting.

## What Changes

- **The hatch marks the one line or region a sentence is about.** A region
  named as the subject ("this cage", "the outlined area") is striped, and the
  outline keeps to particular cells. Where a sentence names a second region, it
  names it by where it lies or by its mark, as the line rule already does.
- **No line is named by a number the board does not show.** Boats says "this
  row" over its striped line; Sixteen and Netslide name where a tile goes by the
  mark already on its destination rather than by row or column number; the line
  a Netslide slide cannot use is "this column", striped.
- Wording that named a region by its outline ("the outlined region", "the
  outlined galaxy", "the outlined area", "the outlined run", "the shaded group",
  "the outlined block") names it by its stripes.

Census, 2026-09-23 (every hinting game's plans, every leaf preset, two seeds;
keyed on the words and then classified by hand):

- **Numbered lines**: Boats, Sixteen, Netslide.
- **Regions as subject**: Keen (cage), Solo (block; its diagonals are lines and
  already striped), Filling (region), Palisade (region), Galaxies (galaxy), Rome
  (area, group), Seismic (area), Crossing (run), Range (run), Tracks (block).
- **Not regions**: Boats' "this segment" and Bridges' "the outlined island" name
  one piece; Salad's "the outlined square" is a particular square.

## Impact

- The named games' hints and renderers; `docs/games/hints.md` § "Hatch the line
  the sentence names" generalizes to regions; the cross-game guard's population.
- Player-visible: marks and wording. Owner-requested.
