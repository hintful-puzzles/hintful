## REMOVED Requirements

### Requirement: A tier too rare to deal says so

**Reason**: A size is no longer refused for being rare or slow ("A rare tier
is dealt under the deadline"). A line drawn from deal times moves with every
solver and every device, and the deadline, the Stop control and the sentence
for a deal that found no board answer a rare size the same way in every game.

**Migration**: A size refused as too rare to deal is dealt. `tooRareToDeal`
is removed.

## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: A rare tier is dealt under the deadline

A tier or size that is merely *rare* or slow SHALL NOT be refused: it is
generated by retrying under the engine's deadline, and the game states no
bound for it. A size SHALL be refused only where no board exists, shown by a
count or a proof ("A refusal that claims absence rests on a count"), or for a
structural limit such as memory or an encoding, and never by a bound drawn
from deal times or acceptance rates.

#### Scenario: A rare tier that deals promptly is dealt

- **WHEN** a size has boards at a tier that the generator finds only after
  many tries, each try taking a small fraction of a second in total
- **THEN** the app's deal keeps trying and deals a board that needs that tier
- **AND** the generator states no count of tries for it

#### Scenario: A rare tier that takes seconds is dealt

- **WHEN** a size has boards found once in a measured number of tries, taking
  seconds or minutes a board, or that the deal gives up on more often than it
  finds
- **THEN** parameter validation accepts it when a board is to be dealt
- **AND** the deal runs out only where the deadline passes first
