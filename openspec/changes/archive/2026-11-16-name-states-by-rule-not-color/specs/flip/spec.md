## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Hint: Flip's hint calls the two states lit and unlit

Flip's hint sentences and hint-mark legends SHALL say "lit" and "unlit" for a
square's two states, and its help page SHALL name the two pieces by the pair's
placeholder, which says each piece's shape.

#### Scenario: The help page names the pieces

- **WHEN** a player reads Flip's help page
- **THEN** an unlit square's piece and a lit square's are each named by the
  pair's placeholder, and by no color word written in the page
