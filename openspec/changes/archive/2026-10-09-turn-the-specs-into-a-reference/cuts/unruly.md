| "Unruly game implements the Game interface" (whole requirement and its scenario): that a registered `unruly` game implements `Game`, provides `solve` and `textFormat`, and has no `statusbarText` | type | The compiler and the registry say it of every game; `ts-engine` "Solve, the status bar and text export follow from the game's methods" reads the methods' presence. | "Unruly's presets are four sizes and one unique board" (whole requirement and its scenario) | declared | `presets()` in `src/games/unruly/state.ts`, which the engine reads; the menu's shape is `engine-params` "The preset menu is a grid". |
