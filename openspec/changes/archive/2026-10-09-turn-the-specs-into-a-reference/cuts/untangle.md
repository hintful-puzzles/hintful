# Cuts: untangle

Requirements: 28 before, 28 after. No requirement went whole; the cuts are sentences and scenario clauses.

| Requirement or sentence cut | Word | What holds it now, or why it is gone |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "Untangle game implements the Game interface": that a registered `untangle` game implements `Game<…>`, and provides `solve` and a `hint` hook | type | The compiler and the registry say it; `solve` and `hint` are fields of `untangleGame` in `src/games/untangle/index.ts`. The decision that stays (no `findMistakes`, `statusbarText` or `textFormat`, and why) is retitled "Crossed edges are Untangle's only mistake feedback". |
| "Untangle game implements the Game interface": "a planar graph of `n` vertices joined by edges, drawn tangled, solved when ... no two edges cross" | duplicate | "Generation yields a planar graph drawn tangled" and "Crossing detection is exact and drives solved status". |
| "Untangle's params are a vertex count": "Presets of 6, 10, 15, 20 and 25 vertices SHALL be offered, and the default SHALL be `n = 10`", and the presets clause of its round-trip scenario | declared | `presets` and `defaultParams` of `untangleGame`. The encoding and the bounds stay. |
| "Generation yields a planar graph drawn tangled": "points scattered on a grid, edges added greedily lowest-degree-vertex-first, each accepted only when it crosses no existing point and no existing edge", and "in a shuffled order, re-rolled until" | how | The construction is `newUntangleDesc` in `src/games/untangle/generator.ts`. What it promises stays: planar, degree at most 4, on a circle, never starting solved. |
| "Crossing detection is exact and drives solved status": "The crossing set SHALL be recomputed on every state transition and exposed to `redraw` so crossed edges can be highlighted" | how | Which field caches the crossings (`UntangleState.crosses`). That crossed edges draw red stays in "Rendering frames the play area and colors roles distinctly". |
| "Vertices are dragged by pointer or keyboard": "The editor-only edge add and delete moves SHALL NOT be mapped" | port | Upstream's editor is a separate compile-time build, not a behavior of the game; nothing here could map it. |
| "executeMove places vertices and refuses a malformed move": "The move SHALL be structured-clone-safe, using the default serialize and deserialize" | how | The move is plain numbers and `untangleGame` has no `serializeMove`; what a save depends on stays in "The layout is restored by replaying the move log". |
| "A vertex's color says its role": "The hint SHALL be the collection's hint color" | duplicate | "redraw draws a displayed hint in the hint color". |
| "Solve untangles any planar board, with or without aux": "SHALL be marked as solved-with-help", and the same clause in two scenarios | collection | `ts-engine`, "Solve leaves a solved board" (scenario "Solve finishes the board": the midend reports it solved-with-help) and "The engine derives a board's history from its position". |
| "The solved layout is exact and checked": "a planarity embedding and a straight-line grid drawing, spread by a relaxation that never lets it tangle" | how | The algorithm is `src/games/untangle/planar.ts` and `solution.ts`. The promise stays: `aux` when there is one, otherwise from the edges alone, exact, and checked crossing-free. |
| "Untangle hints move the point that removes the most crossings": "The `untangle` game SHALL implement the `hint` hook" | type | `hint` is a field of `untangleGame`. |
| "Untangle's hint refusals": "A hint on a solved board SHALL be refused with the collection's already-solved wording, by the midend before it asks the game", and its scenario | collection | `engine-hints`, "The midend SHALL refuse a hint on a finished or wrong board before asking the game". The game's own refusal stays, with a scenario for it (a hand-typed K5), which the requirement lacked. |
