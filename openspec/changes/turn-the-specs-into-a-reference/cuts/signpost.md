# Cuts: signpost

Requirements: 20 before, 14 after.

| Requirement or sentence cut | Word | What holds it now, or why it is gone |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "Signpost game implements the Game interface": that the engine provides a registered `signpost` game implementing `Game`, and that it provides `solve` and `textFormat` | type | The compiler and the registry say it; `solve` and `textFormat` are fields of `signpostGame` in `src/games/signpost/index.ts`, and `ts-engine` "Solve, the status bar and text export follow from the game's methods" derives the rest. The rules of the puzzle stay, retitled "Signpost links every square into one numbered chain". |
| "Signpost's presets" (whole): 4×4, 4×4 free ends, 5×5, 5×5 free ends, 6×6 and 7×7, and the scenario "Both kinds of 4×4 are offered" | declared | `SIGNPOST_PRESETS` in `src/games/signpost/index.ts`, which the engine reads; the requirement held no rule about the list. |
| "Signpost maintains the linked-chain state model" (whole): the `next`/`prev` links, the disjoint-set forest, the derived number and color group recomputed on every move, state immutable and cloned per move | how | Which fields `SignpostState` holds and when they are recomputed (`src/games/signpost/state.ts`). What a player sees of it stays in "Signpost's region colors follow the links", which takes the scenario "Linking renumbers a region". |
| "Signpost exposes the victory-flash preference": "through the `Game.prefs` hook" | type | `prefs` is a field of `signpostGame`; `ts-engine` "The engine supports per-game user preferences". |
| "Signpost exposes the victory-flash preference": "and SHALL persist through the standard preferences mechanism" | collection | `ts-engine` "A preference survives a new board" and "Preferences are not part of a save" (persisted per puzzle by the settings store). |
| "Signpost generates solver-gated boards reproducibly": "That SHALL hold of each of its stages: the random walk that grows the path from its head and its tail, the selection of clues in a shuffled order gated by the solver, and the final encoding" | how | The construction is `newSignpostDesc` in `src/games/signpost/generator.ts`. What it promises stays: the same desc from the same seed, and clues enough for the solver. |
| "Signpost's palette carries four ramps over the region colors" (whole), and the scenario "Region colors repaint after linking" | declared | `buildPalette` in `src/games/signpost/render.ts` and the `SIGNPOST_*` ramps it reads from `src/engine/color/palette-games.ts`. That a square takes its region's color, and which color survives a merge, stay in "Signpost's region colors follow the links" and "Signpost draws its squares on the collection's quiet surface". |
| "Signpost repaints a square only when what it shows changed" (whole): the first draw, the spin angle, the derived number, the inbound direction and the packed flag word as the repaint key, and every overlay rebuilt each frame | how | The tile cache of `redrawSignpost`; `engine-drawing` "A warm frame matches a fresh paint of the same state" holds what a player would see of a missing key input, for every registered game. Its one visible rule, that a `findMistakes` flag draws the cell in the error styling, moved into "Signpost reports mistakes for Check & Save" with its scenario. |
| "Signpost's drag sprite uses a blitter": "drawn as a sprite over a blitter: the pixels under it SHALL be saved before it is drawn and restored before the next frame paints" | how | The blitter in `redrawSignpost`. What the drag shows stays, retitled "An arrow follows a Signpost drag": an arrow at the pointer that leaves nothing behind. |
| "Signpost's win flash spins the arrows": "The win flash SHALL spin the arrows, honoring the `flash-type` preference: every arrow one way when unidirectional, and alternate squares in opposite ways when meshing gears" | duplicate | "Signpost exposes the victory-flash preference", which already said the preference changes the pattern of spin directions and now carries this sentence in its place. |
| "Signpost's win flash spins the arrows": "The flash SHALL NOT play after Solve", and the scenario "Solve does not spin" | collection | `ts-engine` "The win flash plays on a forward move that solves the board" (scenario "The Solve command does not celebrate"). |
| "Signpost paints the grid frame on the first draw" (whole) | collection | `engine-drawing` "The game paints everything above the ground" (one-time setup such as the board border, on the first frame of a fresh draw state). How the frame looks stays in "Signpost draws its squares on the collection's quiet surface": no heavier than the grid line. |
