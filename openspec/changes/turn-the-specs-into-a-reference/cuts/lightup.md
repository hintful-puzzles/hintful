# Cuts: lightup

| "Light Up game implements the Game interface" (whole requirement): that a registered `lightup` game implements `Game<…>`, provides `solve` and `textFormat`, and drives a solve-completion flash | type | The compiler and the registry say it of every game; `ts-engine` "Solve, the status bar and text export follow from the game's methods" and "The win flash plays on a forward move that solves the board" read the methods. The puzzle's rules it also stated, and its scenario "A bulb lights its row and column", moved into "Light Up is solved exactly while the grid is correct". |
| "Light Up's presets" (whole requirement and its scenario) | declared | `PRESETS` and `presets()` in `src/games/lightup/state.ts`, which the engine reads. |

| "Light Up ships an explained deductive hint": "No displayed step SHALL be a generic, un-narrated fallback." | collection | `engine-hints` "A hint step always names a technique, with no un-narrated fallback". The requirement's scenario still holds an Easy or Normal plan to no un-narrated step. |
| "A Light Up hint is refused on a solved board and on a wrong one" (whole requirement and its scenario) | collection | `engine-hints` "The midend SHALL refuse a hint on a finished or wrong board before asking the game", which makes both refusals for every game and forbids a game to write either. |
| "No non-Unreasonable Light Up tier requires guessing" (whole requirement and its two scenarios) | collection | `ts-migration` "Narratable-deduction generation policy" and `engine-hints` "Only a tier named Unreasonable requires Search". | "Normal adds the overlapping-set discount": "enumerated with `Combi`" and "Candidates SHALL be those that rule out the member with the fewest rule-outs." | how | Which helper enumerates, and a pruning of the candidates that changes no verdict. | "Unreasonable adds depth-capped recursion": that the recursion picks "the candidate square that would light the most unlit squares", and "Solution counts SHALL be summed across branches." | how | The branching heuristic and the bookkeeping; the depth cap, the "unknown" verdict and the two-solutions scenario stay. |
| "The solver records the clues it used and serves solve and findMistakes": "The solver SHALL track which clue numbers it used, for the generator's stripping. | "Light Up generates solver-gated boards": "with a random draw for the center square of an odd 4-way-rotational grid" and "by filling every open square and then removing lights in a marked sweep" | how | The way the grid and the bulbs are built; the symmetry, the numbering, the solver gate and reproducibility stay. |
| "The generator strips clues while the puzzle stays good" (merged into "Light Up generates solver-gated boards"): the unused-numbers-first pass, "in an order shuffled once for the whole generation", and the scenario's last clause "and the next one in the order is tried" | how | The order of the stripping. That each removal is kept only while the puzzle stays good, the reject of a board solvable one tier lower, and the rest of the scenario stay in the merged requirement. |
| "The impossible-mark is the ruled-out cross": that the preference "SHALL be declared through the `Game.prefs` hook" | type | `ts-engine` "The engine supports per-game user preferences": the hook is the only way a game has one. The key `show-lit-blobs` and its default stay. |
| "Light Up's tile cache keys on one packed word" (whole requirement and its scenario) | how | Which field caches what; `docs/games/rendering.md` § "The tile cache and the diff key" and § "Overlay sidecars" state the rule for every game. "Light Up ships findMistakes" still requires the overlay to repaint on the frame it is computed. |
