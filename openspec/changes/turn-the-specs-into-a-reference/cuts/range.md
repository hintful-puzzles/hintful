# Cuts: range

Requirements: 31 before, 27 after.

| Requirement or sentence cut | Word | What holds it now, or why it is gone |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "Range game implements the Game interface" (whole requirement): that a registered `range` game implements `Game`, provides `solve` and `textFormat`, and has no `statusbarText` | type | The compiler and the registry say it of every game; `ts-engine` "Solve, the status bar and text export follow from the game's methods" reads the methods' presence. The puzzle's rules it also stated, and its scenario "A clue counts itself once", moved into "Range is solved exactly while it has no error". |
| "Range is solved exactly while it has no error": "judged from the board however it was reached" | collection | `ts-engine` "A game's status is judged from the board alone". |
| "Range params are a width and a height": "Four presets SHALL be offered, each taller than wide: 6×9, 8×12, 9×13 and 11×16." | declared | `PRESETS` in `src/games/range/state.ts`, which the engine reads; that each is taller than wide is `engine-params` "Every default and preset draws no wider than tall". |
| "Range params are refused outside their bounds": "The width and height items SHALL each declare a lower bound of 1, from which the engine refuses a non-positive dimension", and the scenario clause on a non-positive dimension | declared | `bounds: { min: 1 }` on `paramConfig` in `state.ts`; `engine-params` "Params validity is the engine's check" refuses from an item's bounds. The two limits `validateParams` holds stay. |
| "Range descriptions are run-length clue grids": "`newState` SHALL parse the desc into the grid with clue cells holding their value and every other cell `EMPTY`." | how | Which values the grid array holds; the encoding and its round-trip stay. |
| "A malformed Range description is refused": "The refusal SHALL be raised by `newState`'s parse, from which the engine derives its verdict on a desc." | collection | `engine-params` "The engine derives a description's verdict from newState". What is refused stays. |
| "Range's generator paints black squares, then strips clues" (whole requirement): paint up to `n / 3` squares, compute clues, strip those opposite a black square, retry, strip pairs in random order | how | The order the generator works in. What it leaves of a board is now in "Range generates uniquely solvable symmetric boards": no clue opposite a black square, no symmetric pair removable without search. Its scenario moved there. |
| "Range's keyboard cursor marks white with shift": "The cursor SHALL be the collection's shared `(x, y)` shape, so `cursor.x` is this game's column and `cursor.y` its row, while the row-major grid's own helpers take `(r, c)`." | how | Which field holds which axis, said on `RangeUi` in `state.ts`; the shared shape is `engine-input` "One keyboard-cursor vocabulary across games". |
| "A Range hint is refused on a solved or mistaken board": the refusals on a solved board and on a board with a mistake, and the scenario for them | collection | `engine-hints` "The midend SHALL refuse a hint on a finished or wrong board before asking the game". | "A known-white Range cell is told without a fill" (whole requirement and its scenario) | duplicate | "Range draws a shaded cell as the collection's shaded piece" (a white mark is the cross with no fill, an undecided cell the plain surface) and "A Range clue sits on the lifted surface of a given". |
| "Range's cursor and hint marks sit at the cell's edge": "and every hint mark" | collection | `engine-hints` "A hint mark is drawn on the cell's border"; "The forced cell and its premises take different marks" also says the ring is at the edge. | "Range names no hue of its own" (whole requirement and its scenario) | collection | `engine-colors` "A game that uses the pair names no hue or shape of its own": hint sentences, control words and the help page's placeholder, for every game that uses the pair. |
