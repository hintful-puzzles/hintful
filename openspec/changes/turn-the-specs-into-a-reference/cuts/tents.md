# Cuts: tents

Requirements: 31 before, 26 after.

| Requirement or sentence cut | Word | What holds it now, or why it is gone |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "Tents game implements the Game interface": that the engine provides a registered `tents` game implementing `Game`, that it provides `solve` and `textFormat`, and the scenario "Tents is registered". The rules of the puzzle stay, as the wording of "Tents completion is judged from the board", which is now the first requirement and spells out the one-to-one matching of each tent to an orthogonally adjacent tree. | type | The compiler and the registry say it of every game; `ts-engine` "Every game with Solve is solved by a test" holds Solve. |
| "Tents completion is judged from the board": "Completion SHALL be judged from the board however it was reached" | collection | `ts-engine` "A game's status is judged from the board alone". |
| "Tents completion is judged from the board": "the win flash SHALL NOT play for the Solve command" | collection | `ts-engine` "The win flash plays on a forward move that solves the board" (a forward move other than the Solve command). |
| "Tents' parameters": "The presets SHALL be 8×8, 10×10 and 15×15, each at Easy and at Normal" | declared | `PRESETS` in `src/games/tents/state.ts`, which the engine reads. |
| "Tents' parameters": "declared as the bounds of the dimension fields, so that the engine's check of the params refuses a smaller grid" | collection | `engine-params` "Params validity is the engine's check" and "A rule that a game rejects params is met by the engine's check". The minimum of 4 and its scenario stay. |
| "A malformed Tents description is rejected": the requirement as a separate one. What is refused (an invalid character, a wrong grid area, a missing or malformed number) and the scenario stay, as the last sentence and second scenario of "Tents descriptions use the upstream run-length encoding". | duplicate | Merged into "Tents descriptions use the upstream run-length encoding". |
| "A malformed Tents description is rejected": that `validateDesc` does the rejecting | obsolete | No game declares a validator; `engine-params` "The engine derives a description's verdict from newState" (`descValue(parseDesc(...))` in `state.ts`). |
| "A malformed Tents description is rejected": "`newState` SHALL parse the desc into a tree grid and an edge-number array that every state of the game shares and none writes, with all non-tree squares initially blank" | how | Which fields the state holds and which are shared; the `readonly` fields of `TentsState` say it. |
| "An over-committed group of tents or trees is red": "`redraw` SHALL take two connected-component passes over the adjacency of trees and tents, the second counting a blank square as a possible tent" | how | The two groups each color is judged in stay, stated as what they are; that they are two passes does not matter. |
| "Tents takes a cursor and direct keys": "Arrow keys SHALL move a cursor, revealing it first" | collection | `engine-input` "One arrow press reveals the cursor and moves it" (one press reveals and moves; the old wording said otherwise). |
| "Tents solves with a graded deductive solver" (whole requirement and its scenario) | how | The 0/1/2 verdict codes and which functions call the solver. What it is used for stays in "Tents ships findMistakes" and "Tents generates solver-gated boards reproducibly"; `ts-engine` "Solve leaves a solved board" and "Every game with Solve is solved by a test" hold Solve from a played position. |
| "Tents generates solver-gated boards reproducibly": "at squares taken in a random permutation", "place the trees by the bipartite `matching`", "derive the edge numbers" | how | Which algorithm lays the board out; the tent count, the no-touching rule, the matching, the empty-line rejection and the difficulty gate stay. |
| "Every Tents overlay is in the diff key" (whole requirement and its scenario) | collection | `engine-drawing` "A warm frame matches a fresh paint of the same state", for every registered game; the packed `Int32Array` word is how Tents meets it. |
| "A Tents hint step says why and marks what it uses": "of at most 120 characters" | collection | `engine-hints` "Hint narration SHALL be short enough to read at a glance". |
| "The Tents hint is refused on a solved or mistaken board" (whole requirement and its scenario) | collection | `engine-hints` "The midend SHALL refuse a hint on a finished or wrong board before asking the game", which also forbids the game writing either refusal. That a wrong link is a mistake stays in "A link no pairing holds is a mistake". |
